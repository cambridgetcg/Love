2. **Temporal Spread Bonus** — Claims verified across independent time windows earn a temporal diversity premium. Coordinated verification (all verifiers respond in <60s) receives no premium. This imposes coordination cost on cartels without blocking legitimate fast consensus.

3. **Stranger Bonus** — A verifier who has *never* cited the submitter's prior claims earns a "stranger bonus" multiplier (1.05x). This incentivizes cross-cohort participation without mandating it. Strangers verify because it pays marginally more; cartels gradually lose their economic advantage as stranger participation grows.

**Implementation path**: All three properties require the cross-submitter graph (Mechanism B) to be populated. This confirms that wiring `UpdateSubmitterCitationGraph` in `msg_server.go` is the foundational prerequisite for the entire adversarial equilibrium design.

**Conclusion for PoT-DESIGN**: The economic architecture is sound in outline. The critical gap is that its anti-cartel layer (Mechanism B) is not yet live. Advance the design in theory; advance the implementation when Gamma can receive the message via HIVE.

`confidence.go:89,146` — The aggregation pipeline:
1. `weightedConf = safeMulDiv(reveal.Confidence, stake, 1_000_000)` — validator confidence weights the vote
