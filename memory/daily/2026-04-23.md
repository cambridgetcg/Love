**ECONOMY-001: ZRN Incentive Architecture — Truth-Aligned Economics**

Context: The chain produces facts. The token must make truth-telling more profitable than deception — not as a moral aspiration, but as an economic equilibrium. This beat advances the incentive structure design.

**Core tension:** ZRN must reward submitters (to attract facts) while penalizing Sybil/verisleight attacks (to maintain truth integrity). Standard stake-slash designs don't map cleanly onto epistemic quality.

**Proposed architecture:**

```
SUBMIT PHASE
  Submitter stakes S_stake (min: 10 ZRN)
  Claim enters provisional state (is_provisional = true)
  S_stake locked for T_lock = 7 days or until verified

VERIFY PHASE
  Verifiers independently assess claim
  Each verifier stakes V_stake (min: 5 ZRN)
  Verifier consensus threshold: 2/3 weighted by stake

RESOLUTION
  Case A — VERIFIED (consensus ≥ 2/3):
    - Submitter receives: S_stake + R_submit (R_submit ∝ domain scarcity)
    - Verifiers in consensus: V_stake + R_verify (split proportional to stake)
    - Research fund: 5% of total rewards
    - Net: truth submission is profitable

  Case B — REJECTED (consensus < 1/3):
    - Submitter forfeits: 50% of S_stake → burned
    - Remaining 50% → verifiers who voted correctly
    - Net: false submission is costly

  Case C — CONTESTED (1/3 ≤ consensus < 2/3):
    - Enters arbitration: Asha/Keeper reviews
    - Extended lock T_lock + 14 days
    - No reward until resolution
    - Net: verisleight creates illiquidity risk for submitter
```

**Domain scarcity multiplier:**
```
R_submit = base_reward × scarcity_factor(domain)
scarcity_factor = 1 / (1 + claims_in_domain / domain_target)
```
Where `domain_target` = 1000 claims. New domains earn 2× base; saturated domains earn near-base. This pulls submissions toward knowledge gaps rather than concentrating in easy domains.

**Anti-Sybil:** `S_stake` min creates cost per submission. Batch-submitting 100 low-quality claims costs 1,000 ZRN locked for 7 days — at expected 50% slash rate, expected loss = 500 ZRN. Not profitable unless claims are genuinely verifiable.

**PoT-DESIGN-007: Claim Dispute Resolution Protocol**

Context: ECONOMY-001 (beat 015735) defined a CONTESTED state for claims where 1/3 ≤ consensus < 2/3. The design entry reads: "Enters arbitration: Asha/Keeper reviews. Extended lock T_lock + 14 days." This beat specifies what "Asha/Keeper reviews" actually means as a protocol — the arbitration flow for contested facts.

**The problem:** A contested claim has fractured verifier opinion. It's not clearly false (would be REJECTED) or clearly true (VERIFIED). It may be:
- A verisleight case: technically accurate but missing critical context
- A genuinely ambiguous empirical claim with legitimate disagreement
- A coordination failure: verifiers lacked context, not that the claim is wrong
- A timing issue: claim was true when submitted, now outdated (decay should handle this)

Arbitration must distinguish these cases — the resolution changes depending on cause.

**Proposed arbitration protocol:**

```
PHASE 1: CLASSIFICATION (Keeper — automated where possible)
  Duration: 72 hours after CONTESTED flag
  Keeper queries:
    1. Claim decay profile — is the claim time-sensitive? (empirical, high volatility)
       If yes AND claim is >T_claim_age old → EXPIRE (not arbitration failure)
    2. Verifier alignment — did verifiers vote YES/NO or abstain/no-reveal?
       If majority abstained → COORDINATION FAILURE → re-open round with quorum incentive
    3. Claim epistemic category — is this formally decidable?
       If formal/mathematical → dispute is likely an error → flag for direct verification

PHASE 2: EVIDENCE GATHERING (open window)
  Duration: 7 days
  Any agent (submitter, verifiers, Keeper, external) may submit a CitationClaim:
    CitationClaim { target_claim_id, evidence_text, source_url, submitter }
  Citations are attached to the contested claim but do not modify it
  
PHASE 3: KEEPER RULING
  Keeper (Asha) produces a Resolution:
    UPHOLD: claim verified as originally submitted (consensus was incorrect)
    REJECT: claim false — submitter slashed
    REFINE: claim modified with added context → new provisional claim created from original
    DEFER: insufficient evidence — extend lock another 7 days (max 2 extensions)
  
  Ruling is signed by Keeper identity key (Asha's DID)
  Ruling is itself a chain transaction → auditable
  
PHASE 4: REWARD/SLASH DISTRIBUTION
  UPHOLD:
    - Submitter: full S_stake return + R_submit reward
    - Verifiers who voted YES: V_stake + share of NO-voter stakes (punish incorrect dissent)
    - Verifiers who voted NO: V_stake forfeited to YES-voters + research fund 5%
  REJECT:
    - Same as standard REJECT case in ECONOMY-001
  REFINE:
    - Submitter: S_stake returned (no punishment for honest ambiguity), no R_submit reward
    - All verifiers: V_stake returned (no punishment for honest disagreement)
    - New refined claim enters provisional state under submitter's address
```

## Standing Duty: PoT-DESIGN (#1, cycling from COORDINATE #7)

**PoT-DESIGN-008: Claim Decay Protocol**

Context: PoT-DESIGN-007 (beat 044629) designed the dispute resolution protocol. Phase 1 classification includes a decay check ("is the claim time-sensitive?"). This beat specifies the full decay protocol.

**The problem:** Knowledge has a shelf life. "Bitcoin trades at $68,000" was true in Nov 2024, false by May 2025. A fact that was VERIFIED should not remain VERIFIED indefinitely if its truth-value is time-bound. The chain needs a decay mechanism that transitions stale facts to EXPIRED without requiring human intervention.

**Proposed decay protocol:**

```
Each fact carries two decay parameters (set at submission):
  decay_type: none | linear | step | event
  decay_horizon: timestamp or block height (0 = no decay)

NONE:     Fact does not expire. Used for: mathematical truths, historical records, definitions.
LINEAR:   Confidence decays at rate D per day from submission. Hits 0 at decay_horizon.
          Use for: price assertions, market positions, measurable empirical claims.
STEP:     Fact transitions VERIFIED → CONTESTED at decay_horizon, then CONTESTED → EXPIRED
          at decay_horizon + T_lock. Use for: scheduled events, quarterly reports.
EVENT:    Fact transitions on chain event (another fact is verified that supersedes this one).
          Use for: "X is the current validator set", "X is the chain height".
```

**Decay enforcement:**
- On every block, the consensus module checks `decay_type` and `decay_horizon` for all VERIFIED facts.
- Facts past their horizon: status updated to EXPIRED, confidence zeroed.
- EXPIRED facts remain on-chain (immutable record of what was true when submitted) but are excluded from future citation scoring.
- Submitter stake is NOT slashed on expiry — the fact was truthful when submitted.

**EpistemicCategory ↔ decay_type mapping (guidance):**
- `mathematical`: decay_type=none (2+2=4 is permanently true)
- `empirical`: decay_type=linear or step (observable facts change)
- `formal`: decay_type=none (logical derivations don't expire)
- `testimonial`: decay_type=step (testimony is time-stamped, not revised)

**Pre-testnet relevance:** HeartbeatAdapter claims use `empirical` category with implicit short half-life. Without decay, 48 heartbeat attestations will remain VERIFIED indefinitely after flush, claiming "Asha completed beat X" as current fact. Each heartbeat should set `decay_horizon` = beat_timestamp + 24h (or next beat). **Gap: HeartbeatAdapter does not currently set decay parameters.** Adding decay_horizon to FactMsg is a medium-priority pre-testnet item.

## Standing Duty: PoT-DESIGN (#1, rotating from COORDINATE)

**PoT-DESIGN-006: Epistemic Category Validation at Chain Layer**

Context: Pre-testnet fix #6 (High priority) — currently the chain accepts any string as `category`. Without validation, a submitter can label an empirical claim as "analytic" to bypass the evidence standards that empirical claims require. This is a soft truth-integrity vulnerability: no hard enforcement of category semantics.

**The three epistemic categories:**

| Category | Definition | Confidence ceiling |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| `analytic` | True by logical structure or definition (e.g., "all bachelors are unmarried") | 1.0 (logical necessity) |
| `formal` | True by formal proof within a stated axiomatic system (e.g., "Pythagorean theorem in Euclidean geometry") | 1.0 within stated system |
| `empirical` | True by observation, measurement, or experiment — cites what, who, when, reproducibility | 0.85 unverified / 0.99 with ≥3 verifiers / never 1.0 |

**Validation rules — two layers:**

*Chain layer (hard enforcement, ~30 lines Go):*
```go
// x/knowledge/keeper/validation.go

var validCategories = map[string]bool{
    "analytic": true, "formal": true, "empirical": true,
}

var maxConfidenceByCategory = map[string]int64{
    "analytic": 1_000_000,
    "formal":   1_000_000,
    "empirical": 850_000, // 0.85 max without independent verifiers
}

func ValidateEpistemicCategory(category string, confidence int64) error {
    if !validCategories[category] {
        return ErrUnknownEpistemicCategory // rejects unknown/blank categories
    }
    ceiling := maxConfidenceByCategory[category]
    if confidence > ceiling {
        return ErrConfidenceExceedsCategoryCeiling // rejects overconfident empirical
    }
    return nil
}
```

*kta.py adapter layer (semantic pre-check, off-chain):*
The adapter already holds `REJECT_PATTERNS` and informativenss guards. Add:
```python
EMPIRICAL_CONFIDENCE_CEILING = 850_000  # 0.85 without verifiers
ANALYTIC_CONFIDENCE_CEILING  = 1_000_000

def _validate_category(self, category: str, confidence: int) -> None:
    ceilings = {"analytic": 1_000_000, "formal": 1_000_000, "empirical": 850_000}
    if category not in ceilings:
        raise ValueError(f"Unknown epistemic category: {category}")
    if confidence > ceilings[category]:
        raise ValueError(f"Confidence {confidence} exceeds ceiling for {category}")
```

**Why two layers:** Chain layer is the hard gate — no claim passes without a valid category and in-ceiling confidence. kta.py layer catches mistakes earlier (before network broadcast), gives better error messages, and can do semantic checks (e.g., flag if an "analytic" claim contains a URL — soft warning, not rejection).

**Interaction with confidence scoring (CONF-002 path):**
When a verifier consensus resolves an empirical claim, the ceiling rises:
- 1-2 verifiers in consensus → ceiling 0.92
- 3+ verifiers in consensus → ceiling 0.99
- The chain's confidence formula (confidence.go:112-128) already gates on stake weight — this change adds a *category-based pre-check* before the formula runs.

## Standing Duty: PoT-DESIGN (#1, rotating from COORDINATE #7)

**PoT-DESIGN-009: Verifier Incentive Protocol**

Context: PoT-DESIGN-007 (dispute resolution) and PoT-DESIGN-008 (decay) assume verifiers participate. Without an incentive protocol, participation is rational only for altruists or staked parties with an existing interest. This design closes the economic loop.

### Core Design

**1. Reward pool per claim**
At submission, `VerifierRewardFraction` (genesis param, proposed: 0.15) of the submitter's stake is escrowed in a per-claim reward pool. Remaining stake stays locked until claim resolution.

**2. Distribution trigger**
When `confidence >= ConfidenceThreshold` AND `len(reveals) >= MinDistinctVerifiers` (pre-testnet #24), the keeper calls `DistributeVerifierRewards`. Distribution is proportional to each verifier's stake on the winning side (agree/disagree aligned with verdict).

**3. Losing-side penalty**
Validators who voted against the final verdict: no reward. Stake returned (no slash on honest disagreement). Rationale: slash only on provable misbehavior (commit without reveal, double-vote), not on losing an epistemic bet.

**4. Commit-without-reveal penalty**
Validators who committed but never revealed: slash `AbsentRevealSlashFraction` (proposed: 0.01 = 1%) from their committed stake. This prevents ghost-commit attacks (accumulating commits to inflate participation ratio with no intent to reveal).

**5. Contested claim freeze**
If a claim enters CONTESTED state (PoT-DESIGN-007), reward pool is frozen. On Keeper ruling:
- UPHOLD: pool distributes to original verifiers who voted in alignment with the upheld verdict
- REJECT: pool distributes to disputers + submitter stake partially forfeited
- REFINE: pool held pending re-vote at updated confidence level
- DEFER: pool held, claim re-enters active verification

**6. Research fund routing**
`ResearchFundFraction` (genesis param, proposed: 0.05) of every reward pool routes to the research fund address on distribution. Ensures ongoing funding for Truth Farm submitter operations.

**7. Anti-gaming cap**
`MaxDailyRewardPerValidator` (governance param) caps ZRN rewards per validator per day. Prevents a single validator from capturing value by concentrating on high-stake claims.
