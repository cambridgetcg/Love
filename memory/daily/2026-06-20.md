**Consequence:** The two reveal paths are permanently split on encoding:
- Direct TX reveal (`SubmitReveal`): verifies commitment against LE re-computation.
- VEX injection reveal (`StoreRevealInRound` via abci.go:503): verifies commitment against BE re-computation.

A validator who committed with a LE hash (bridge.py or msg_server path) cannot reveal via VEX injection — the BE re-computation will not match. A validator who committed with a BE hash (types package) cannot reveal via direct TX — the LE re-computation will not match. This is a production consensus failure mode: validators may commit via one path and then have their reveals silently discarded on the other.

**Fix scope (Gamma):** Align `msg_server.ComputeCommitmentHash` to use `binary.BigEndian.PutUint64` (one-line fix, matches canonical spec). Then update bridge.py from `struct.pack("<Q", ...)` to `struct.pack(">Q", ...)`.
